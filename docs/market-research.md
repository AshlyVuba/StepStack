## 4. StepStack Pricing and Unit Economics

**Unit economics** means the money earned and spent for each paying learner or seat.

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| B2C | We sell directly to individual learners |
| B2B | We sell to organisations |
| Seat | One learner's paid place |
| MRR / ARR | Recurring revenue per month / per year |
| Variable cost | A cost that grows with each learner |
| Fixed cost | A cost that stays about the same as learner numbers change |
| Contribution margin | Price minus variable cost for one learner |
| Break-even | The number of learners needed to cover fixed costs |
| CAC | Customer acquisition cost: the cost of winning one customer |
| Churn | The share of customers who stop paying in a period |
| LTV | Lifetime value: the margin expected from a customer before they leave |

### 4.1 Formulas

- Monthly revenue = price per month × paying learners or seats
- Annual revenue = monthly revenue × 12 (assumes numbers and price stay the same all year)
- Contribution margin = price − variable cost per learner
- Monthly profit = monthly revenue − total variable costs − fixed costs
- Break-even learners = fixed monthly costs ÷ contribution margin (round up; margin must be above zero)
- Simple LTV = monthly contribution margin ÷ monthly churn rate
- Rand amount = US dollar amount × exchange rate (record the rate, date and source)
